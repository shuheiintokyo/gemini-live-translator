Where are my podcasts?

This page can't sign in to Apple Podcasts or reach your iPhone, but on a Mac it can open episodes you've downloaded in the Podcasts app.

  1. Download the episode in the Podcasts app on this Mac.
  2. Click One file.
  3. In the window's sidebar, under Media, click Music, then open Podcasts and the show.
  4. Choose the episode and click Upload or Open. The file stays on your Mac: only the audio goes to Gemini for subtitles.

Episodes from paid subscriptions may not play.

Prepared subtitles use Groq to transcribe and Gemini to translate. The keys are saved on this device only and sent only to Groq and Google.

The Gemini key is the same one the Live page uses, so saving it here updates it there too. Keep both keys private.
